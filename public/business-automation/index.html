What business automation means here

It isn’t about big software or buzzwords. It’s about taking the small, repeated jobs that eat your evenings and making them happen on their own: a customer fills in a form, you get the details in your inbox, they get a reply, and the job lands where it needs to go.

We start with how you already work, then automate the steps that are slow, easy to forget or easy to get wrong. If something is better left as it is, we’ll say so.

Things we can set up

Smart enquiry forms

Forms that ask the right questions for your kind of work, so the first message already has the suburb, the type of job and a way to reach the customer. Every enquiry arrives by email, and your phone number can sit right beside the form for people who’d rather ring. We build these into our websites as standard, like the six-field quote form on a landscaper’s site we built.

Follow-up that doesn’t slip

An automatic reply so the customer knows you got their message, and a nudge to you when an enquiry hasn’t been answered.

Reminders for repeat work

For businesses with regular service customers, such as maintenance or seasonal jobs, reminders sent from the details you already hold so you don’t have to remember who’s due.

Typing it once

Moving details from a form, an email or an invoice into a spreadsheet or your records, so you only enter them once.

Room to grow

If a simple connection turns into something bigger, it can grow into custom software without starting again.

Business automation questions

What can be automated in a small business?

Anything you keep doing by hand in the same way each time. Common examples are quote and enquiry forms that email you the details, automatic replies so customers know you got their message, reminders for repeat work, and moving details from a form into a spreadsheet or your invoicing so you only type them once.

How much does business automation cost?

It depends on what you want connected, so we look at your current process first. You get a fixed price in writing before we start. If you already have a website with us, small additions are often quick.

Do I need a new website first?

No. Some automation sits on your existing website, and some works behind the scenes with the tools you already use, like email, forms and spreadsheets. We start from what you’ve got.

Who is business automation for?

Owner-run businesses that lose time to admin: tradies, service businesses, clubs and small local operators. If you keep copying the same details from one place to another, it’s worth a conversation.

Do you only work with businesses in Forster?

No. We’re based in Forster, and we work with businesses across the Mid North Coast. Most of the work happens by phone and email, and we meet in person for major projects.

Doing the same admin over and over?

Tell us what takes up your time. We’ll tell you what we’d automate first, and what we’d leave alone.
