From quick wins to whole systems

It isn’t about buzzwords. It’s about taking the repeated work that eats your evenings and making it happen on its own, whether that’s one form that emails you the details or a system that runs a whole part of the business.

We start with how you already work, then automate or build what’s slow, easy to forget or easy to get wrong. If something is better left as it is, we’ll say so.

Everyday automation

Smart enquiry forms

Forms that ask the right questions for your kind of work, so the first message already has the suburb, the type of job and a way to reach the customer. Every enquiry arrives by email, and your phone number can sit right beside the form for people who’d rather ring. We build these into our websites, like the six-field quote form on a landscaper’s site we built.

Follow-up that doesn’t slip

An automatic reply so the customer knows you got their message, and a nudge to you when an enquiry hasn’t been answered.

Reminders for repeat work

For businesses with regular service customers, such as maintenance or seasonal jobs, reminders sent from the details you already hold so you don’t have to remember who’s due.

Typing it once

Moving details from a form, an email or an invoice into a spreadsheet or your records, so you only enter them once.

Bigger systems and custom software

When off-the-shelf tools don’t fit, we can build software around your business. Things we can take on include:

  • Job and customer tracking. One place for who’s asked for what, what’s booked and what’s been done.
  • Quoting and booking. Quotes, bookings and confirmations that follow your rules, not a generic template.
  • Dashboards and reports. Your numbers in one view, without wrestling a spreadsheet.
  • Customer or member portals. A place for customers to log in, see their details and send you what you need.
  • Connecting your tools. Making your website, email, spreadsheets, invoicing and other systems share details automatically.
  • Purpose-built internal tools. A custom web app for the way your team works, when nothing off the shelf does.

For an example of software we’ve built ourselves, see Tideline, a web app that pulls ocean and tide data onto one map for NSW anglers. We bring the same approach to business systems: show what’s known, keep it clear and make it easy to use.

Automation and software questions

What can be automated in a business?

Anything you keep doing by hand in a repeatable way. That can be small, like enquiry forms that email you the details, automatic replies and reminders for repeat work. It can also be big, like job and customer tracking, quoting, booking, reporting dashboards, or connecting the tools you already use so details move between them on their own.

Can you build custom software for my business?

Yes. When off-the-shelf tools don’t fit how you work, we can build web-based software around your process, such as internal tools, customer or job systems, dashboards and portals. We start by understanding what you do now, then agree what to build in stages.

Is automation only for small tasks?

No. Small, quick wins are a good place to start, but we also take on bigger projects. Tideline, our own coastal-conditions web app, shows the kind of data-driven software we build. See it on our work page.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the job, so we look at your process first. We agree what’s being built and the price in writing before we start, and bigger projects are scoped in stages so you always know what you’re committing to.

Do I need a new website first?

No. Some automation sits on your existing website, and some works behind the scenes with the tools you already use, like email, forms, spreadsheets and invoicing. We start from what you’ve got.

Do you only work with businesses in Forster?

No. We’re based in Forster, and we work with businesses across the Mid North Coast. Most of the work happens by phone and email, and we meet in person for major projects.

Got a job you wish ran itself?

Tell us what takes up your time, or what you wish your business had. We’ll tell you what we’d build or automate first, and what we’d leave alone.
