Case study • Landscaping • Great Lakes NSW

Dan Amato Landscaping

The brief

Dan builds pool surrounds, retaining walls, decking and complete landscapes from Pacific Palms to Rainbow Flat. His previous website listed his services and a phone number, but gave a customer no way to describe a project and ask for a quote online.

What we built

  • A clear one-page website that leads with the work: pool surrounds, retaining walls and decking.
  • Photo galleries made up of Dan’s own completed projects, grouped by service so a customer can browse jobs like theirs.
  • A service-area section naming the towns he works in: Pacific Palms, Forster–Tuncurry, Nabiac and Rainbow Flat.
  • A quote form that collects the customer’s name, phone, email, suburb, type of project and site details, and sends each enquiry by email.
  • Business details such as ABN and licence number in the footer, so customers can see who they are dealing with.

MidCoast Web Co. also looks after the site after launch.

At a glance

  • Client: Dan Amato Landscaping
  • Area: Great Lakes, NSW
  • What we did: website design and build, photo galleries, quote form and ongoing care
  • Services shown: pool landscaping and surrounds, retaining walls, decking and outdoor living

Our own project • Web app • NSW coast

Tideline

What it is

Tideline is a coastal-conditions map for the NSW coast, built for people who fish. It brings the information that matters before a trip onto one screen.

What it does

  • An interactive map with switchable layers, including sea-surface temperature and seabed depth contours with a depth legend.
  • Daily “bite windows” that rank the best times of day using light levels and the tide at the nearest station.
  • Honest weighting: each factor is labelled by how strong the evidence is, and moon phase and pressure count for less because the science on them is mixed.
  • A way to add your own fishing spots to the map.

Tideline is a MidCoast Web Co. project, built to show what we can do with maps, data and clear, honest interfaces.

At a glance

  • Type: our own web app
  • Area: NSW coast, with tide data from the Forster station
  • Built with: TypeScript, hosted on Vercel
  • Features: map layers, depth contours, tide and light-based bite windows, added spots
