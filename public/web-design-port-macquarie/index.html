Port Macquarie web design FAQs

How much does a website cost for a Port Macquarie business?

A starter site is from $1,250, a professional site from $2,500 and a larger build from $4,000, with no GST added. You get a fixed price in writing before work starts, so there are no surprise invoices, and care is from $49 a month after launch.

You’re based in Forster. Can you still build and run my Port Macquarie website?

Yes. Most of the work happens by phone, video call and email, and that suits most projects well. For major projects we meet in person in Port Macquarie. Hosting, updates and changes after launch are all handled remotely, by the same person who built it.

Do you meet clients in Port Macquarie, Laurieton or Wauchope?

Yes, in person for major projects, anywhere from Port Macquarie to Laurieton and Wauchope. For smaller jobs we keep things simple with phone, video and email, so you’re not waiting on a meeting to get started. You still deal with one person throughout.

How long does a new website take?

Typically about a week. Bigger builds take longer, and timing also depends on how quickly your photos and wording come together. We agree the plan and the price first, so you know what’s being built, when and what it costs.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting again?

Often, yes. We look at what’s already working, such as pages that get found or good reviews. Then we keep it. If a rebuild makes more sense, we’ll explain why and quote both options so you can decide what suits the business.

Do you write the content and supply photos?

It depends on you. We can write the words for your pages and add placeholder images to get the site built, or you can supply your own text and photos. Real photos of your own business always work best, so send them when you have them.

Will my site show up in Google and AI search results?

Nobody can honestly guarantee it. We set up the groundwork: a complete Google Business Profile, clear service pages, structured data, a sitemap and site settings that let search tools read your pages. Visibility builds from there, and we keep checking it.

What does website care include, and what does it cost?

Care starts from $49 a month. It covers hosting, security updates, backups, uptime checks and small text or photo changes. If something breaks, we’re the ones who fix it, and you deal with the same person who built the site.
