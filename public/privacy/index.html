Privacy and analytics

What happens to your details when you get in touch, and how our optional analytics work.

Enquiries

MidCoast Web Co. uses the name, email address, business details and message you provide to respond to your enquiry and discuss the work you request. When you send the enquiry form, your details are passed securely to Web3Forms, an email delivery service, which forwards them to us and may keep a copy for a limited time. Web3Forms may process information outside Australia. Your enquiry is not displayed publicly.

Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive documents. To ask about information you have supplied, email hello@midcoastweb.com.au.

Optional analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, this website loads Google Analytics to measure page visits, clicks on email and phone links, and enquiries sent. We do not send the contents of your enquiry to Analytics. Sending an enquiry is recorded as a separate event; the contents of your enquiry are not.

Google Analytics uses cookies and processes technical information about your visit. Our website sends the page address without its query string or fragment and sends only the referring website origin. Google may process information outside Australia. Read Google’s privacy policy.

If you decline, the website does not load its Google Analytics tag. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. External services, including Web3Forms, have their own privacy settings.

Change analytics choice

If analytics is currently enabled, this control switches it off and reloads the page. Use the control again to choose whether to enable it.
