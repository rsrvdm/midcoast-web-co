Privacy and analytics

How this website handles enquiries and optional website analytics.

Enquiries

MidCoast Web Co. uses the name, email address, business details and message you provide to respond to your enquiry and discuss the work you request. The enquiry form is hosted by Google Forms and responses are stored in our Google account. They are not displayed publicly.

Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive documents. To ask about information you have supplied, email hello@midcoastweb.com.au.

Optional analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, this website loads Google Analytics to measure page visits and clicks on email, phone and enquiry-form links. We do not send the contents of your enquiry to Analytics. Opening the form is measured separately from submitting it; submitted enquiries are recorded in Google Forms.

Google Analytics uses cookies and processes technical information about your visit. Our website sends the page address without its query string or fragment and sends only the referring website origin. Google may process information outside Australia. Read Google’s privacy policy.

If you decline, the website does not load its Google Analytics tag. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. External services, including Google Forms, have their own privacy settings.

Change analytics choice

If analytics is currently enabled, this control switches it off and reloads the page. Use the control again to choose whether to enable it.
