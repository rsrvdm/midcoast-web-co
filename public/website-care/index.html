Website Care That Keeps Your Site Useful

Updates, monitoring, backups and quick fixes, so your site keeps working while you get on with running the business.

Send an enquirySee our work

Launch day isn’t the finish line.

Most websites don’t fail on launch day. They fade. Services change, staff move on, software needs updating and customers expect today’s information, not last year’s. Website care gives all of that a clear owner, so it doesn’t sit at the bottom of your to-do list.

MidCoast Web Co. offers practical ongoing support for regional businesses that want dependable help without hiring a web team. The right arrangement depends on the site, the technology behind it and how often your business needs changes.

Care can include

  • Software and security updates
  • Checks on uptime, security and your enquiry form
  • Backup oversight where applicable
  • Small content and image changes
  • Speed and broken-link reviews
  • Analytics and Search Console checks

Look after what you’ve already built

Keep things current

Update services, team details, opening hours and calls to action before out-of-date information costs you trust, or enquiries.

Catch problems early

Regular checks can spot a failed form, a broken link, an expired connection or a slow page before a customer tells you about it.

Keep improving

Search and enquiry data can point to small, useful changes, instead of leaving the site untouched for years.

Support that fits your site

Some brochure websites need only light monitoring and the odd edit. Others lean on forms, booking tools, changing content or third-party services and need a closer eye. We review your current setup first, so the scope is clear and there are no vague promises.

Care is especially valuable after a redesign. It keeps performance, local SEO and enquiries moving in the same direction. New service pages can go up as the business grows, project evidence gets published properly, and important technical changes don’t have to wait until something breaks.

If we didn’t build your website, we can still take a look. We’ll check the platform, access, hosting and current condition before confirming what support makes sense. If the site needs major repair work, we’ll say so separately rather than burying it inside a maintenance plan.
