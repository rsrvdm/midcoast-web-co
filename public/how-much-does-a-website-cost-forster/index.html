How much does a website cost in Forster?

Short answer: most small local businesses spend between $1,250 and $4,000 to get a proper website, then a small monthly amount to keep it healthy. Here’s what sits behind those numbers, so you can decide what’s worth it.

Get a fixed quoteSee our work

Three common starting points

Every business is different, so treat these as honest “from” prices rather than a rate card. Once we know what you need, you get a fixed price in writing before any work starts.

No surprise invoices halfway through, and no paying for pages you’ll never use.

Quick guide

  • Starter site: from $1,250
  • Professional site: from $2,500
  • Larger or custom build: from $4,000
  • Website care: from $49 a month

What you get at each level

Starter site, from $1,250

A clean, mobile-friendly site of around five pages for a business that mainly needs to look credible and be easy to contact. Your services, your area, a few photos, and a clear way to call or enquire.

Professional site, from $2,500

More pages, more detail and more to rank for. Separate pages for each service, a project gallery, reviews, an enquiry or quote form, and the local search groundwork that helps customers find you.

Larger or custom build, from $4,000

For businesses with bigger needs: lots of services or locations, booking or quoting tools, or a design that has to do something a template can’t. Quoted per project.

What changes the price

Most of the difference between a $1,250 site and a $4,000 one comes down to the number of pages, how much writing and photography needs to be sorted out, and whether you need extras like a quote form, a gallery or integrations with tools you already use.

Having your photos, logo and a rough list of services ready also keeps the cost down. If you’re starting from scratch, we can help you work out what to include and what to leave out.

Keeping it healthy: from $49 a month

A website isn’t a one-off purchase. Hosting, security updates, backups and the odd small change all need looking after. Our care plans start from $49 a month and cover hosting, security, backups, uptime checks and small text or photo changes, so your site stays fast, safe and current without you having to think about it.

You can read more on our website care page.

Is the cheapest option the best value?

Not always. A very cheap site that’s slow on phones, vague about what you do, or invisible on Google costs you in missed enquiries. A fair price gets you something built around your business, with a clear path from a Google search to a phone call.
