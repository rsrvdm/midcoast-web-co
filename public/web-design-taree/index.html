Common questions

How much does a website cost for a Taree business?

Do you have an office in Taree, or can we meet in person?

We don’t have a Taree office. We’re in Forster, under an hour away. Smaller jobs run fine by phone, video and email. For major projects we’ll meet you in person in Taree. Either way, you deal with the same person start to finish.

How long does a new website take?

Typically about a week. Bigger builds take longer, and it depends on how fast your photos and wording come together. We agree the plan and the price first, so you know what’s happening, when it’s happening and what it costs.

Can you fix or refresh my existing website instead of rebuilding it?

Often, yes. We start with what’s already working, like pages that get found or good reviews, and build on that. If a rebuild makes more sense, we’ll tell you why and quote both, so you can weigh them up and decide.

Will my website show up on Google in Taree?

Nobody can honestly guarantee that. What we can do is get the groundwork right: a complete Google Business Profile, clear service pages, the towns you cover and a steady flow of reviews. Visibility builds from there, and we keep an eye on it.

Do you handle hosting and updates after launch?

Yes. Care plans start from $49 a month. They cover hosting, security updates, backups, uptime checks and small text or photo changes. You don’t have to think about any of it, and if something breaks, we’re the ones who fix it.

Yes. We work right across the Manning Valley, from Wingham out to Old Bar and Harrington. Trades and clubs are two of the groups we build for most. Same fixed prices, and for major projects we’ll meet you in Taree.

You do, once you’ve made full payment. From then on the website and the domain belong to you, not us. It’s your business with your name on it, so it’s only fair that you own it outright. Simple as that.
